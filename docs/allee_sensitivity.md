# Allee Threshold Sensitivity Analysis

## Results (5 seeds, 80 years, by Allee threshold)

| Allee | BC Fjords | AK North | WA/OR | Salish | S. CA |
|-------|-----------|----------|-------|--------|-------|
| 10    | 33.1%     | 19.2%    | 0.7%  | 2.3%   | 0.0%  |
| 25    | 13.5%     | 14.0%    | 0.0%  | 0.0%   | 0.0%  |
| 50    | 13.5%     | 13.7%    | 0.0%  | 0.0%   | 0.0%  |
| 100   | 7.8%      | 8.1%     | 0.0%  | 0.0%   | 0.0%  |
| 500   | 0.2%      | 0.2%     | 0.0%  | 0.0%   | 0.0%  |

## Interpretation

1. **Fjord pocket refugia are robust** — BC Fjords and SE AK North persist at Allee ≤ 100.
   The pocket populations (~300-500 adults/site × 4 sites = 1200-2000 total) stay well above
   Allee thresholds of 25-100.

2. **Non-refugia recovery is an Allee artifact** — WA/OR and Salish only recover at Allee=10,
   which is unrealistically low for broadcast spawners. At Allee ≥ 25, they go extinct.

3. **Coast recolonization fails** — Even with pocket larval export, coast sites receive too few
   settlers to exceed Allee threshold. Settlers arrive at ~5-10/site, far below 50.

4. **Allee=50 is the defensible default** — gives 13.5% fjord recovery (biologically plausible),
   zero non-refugia recovery (honest).

## Implication

Recovery in non-fjord areas (observed in real data) must come from mechanisms not currently
modeled at sufficient strength:
- **Resistance evolution** (in model but insufficient to rescue <50 pop sites)
- **Cryptic deep-water refugia** (not modeled)
- **Behavioral aggregation** reducing effective Allee threshold locally
- **Larval subsidy from multiple fjord regions** concentrating settlers

## Key question for human

Are the observed non-fjord recoveries:
(a) Adjacent to fjord regions (larval spillover from refugia)?
(b) In deep-water or otherwise protected sites?
(c) Widespread across open coast (suggesting resistance evolution)?

This determines whether to add cryptic refugia or boost resistance evolution.
