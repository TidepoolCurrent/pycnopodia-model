   - SPRING (1): Mar-May - Larval settlement

   - FALL (3): Sep-Nov - Disease persistence

3. Biological realism:
   - Temperature cycles seasonally (5°C amplitude)
   - Disease peaks in summer (90% prevalence)
   - Disease suppressed in winter (50% transmission rate)
   - Winter spawning → Spring settlement

config = GeoConfig(n_years=80)
